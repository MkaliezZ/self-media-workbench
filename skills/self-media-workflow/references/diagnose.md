# Account diagnosis

Use available homepage/biography, continuous recent works, original text or media and explicitly defined data. Identify audience situation, objective, claimed identity, content line, opening, structure, profile handoff and available next action. Cite actual source IDs and short exact anchors.

Missing data restrict effect conclusions but do not prevent text/content review. Public likes do not reveal plays, retention, advertising or revenue. No-data is not zero. Third-party panels have coverage/window limits; missing live sales do not prove no account revenue.

Prioritize a few concrete changes by evidence, objective and cost. A mixed topic line is not automatically bad and does not prove why distribution is low. For benchmarks compare relevant conditions and include ordinary/low cases where available; abstract useful structure, never copy another person's history or assume hidden metrics.
