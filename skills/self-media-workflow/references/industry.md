# Industry editing recipes

Use for an industry template or editing style. Read current context and GET `/api/visual/templates`; select using the video goal, actual footage and available materials. Task-entry candidates are keyword hints. An existing project's saved plan and explicit user choice take precedence over a new keyword guess.

Seven versioned recipes cover education, finance/business, beauty services, products, food/local visits, home/property and narrated travel Vlog. Read the chosen recipe's roles, material requirements, exclusions, layouts and emphasis rules. For another industry/form or a story that needs different logic, use [recipe-generation.md](recipe-generation.md). These are single-source local 2D recipes, without multi-source automatic shot selection, tracking, speed ramps or music generation.

1. Inspect real frames/audio, review the transcript/cuts and save explicit caption_groups. Preserve qualifying words and meaningful holds. Assign every retained ASR group once to ordered semantic beats matching that recipe. Do not reorder footage or invent a missing evidence/limitations section to fit. If material does not fit, use a different recipe or the generic visual workflow and explain the actual gap.
2. Discover `editing-template`. Bind project/review/run/source, template id/version, selection_reason and an actual frame_inspection_note. Each beat identifies role, consecutive word_ids, reason, material_note and source/image mode. Adjacent beats may share a role; caption groups cannot straddle beats. The service checks structure, not actual truth.
3. Image beats need a real local asset, hash, nature and usage declaration. Inspect the source picture before selecting its highlight. Supply protected_regions for faces, key actions and UI in normalized source-frame coordinates. Default slots vary by industry and orientation. If a slot overlaps a protected region, choose a safe slot_override or original footage; the user need not choose coordinates.
4. Compile through compile_editing_template. This returns a draft, not a saved plan or finished video; attempts remain in media/<id>/template-attempts. Check placement, whole-sentence emphasis, material relevance and caption safety. Revise beats and recompile; do not directly change operations while retaining a template claim.
5. Save the resulting visual_plan unchanged through visual_plan_save. The service checks recipe hash and recomputes operations, then freezes the definition/assets/font. Refresh context and export with visual_plan_id; poll to done, decode output and inspect representative frames/audio. Compilation is not completed rendering or semantic acceptance.
6. Resume from context.visual_plans[].request.template. Source/review changes require reassessing anchors and compiling/saving a new plan. Template upgrades do not rewrite old plans/exports. Preserve failures and revisions.

Do not fabricate before/after effects, prices, financial data, experiences, measurements or service outcomes. Illustration and actual evidence remain distinct. Technical fixtures do not establish industry production quality or publishing results.
