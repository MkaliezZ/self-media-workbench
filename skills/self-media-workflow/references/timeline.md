# Native visual/audio timeline

Use for silent footage, multiple sources, travel Vlog, still-image holds, independently timed voice/original sound/music, or reviewed music cuts. No ASR is required. Use the unified task entry to import video/audio and PNG/JPEG; silent video is accepted, while transcription explicitly rejects it. Do not invent word IDs or append fake speech to make a visual task fit.

Read current context, capabilities, editing_logics and schemas timeline-plan / timeline-export / music-onsets. Existing ASR visual-plan exports remain separate. A custom editing-recipe may declare native_timeline mode, with its actual story/shot/pacing/audio logic; attach it as timeline-plan.recipe. Unsupported tracking, layer animation, time-warp or generated music remain planning-only.

1. Inspect the actual files, source durations, frames, sound and event relationships. Plan scenes by purpose, not one cut per detected onset. Preserve meaningful silent/ambient time. For speech-containing cuts, inspect source audio and actual group bounds first; the native timeline does not claim the word-preserving ASR contract.
2. sources assigns short local IDs to imported media (kind=media, asset_id=context.media[].id) or imported images (kind=image, asset_id=context.project.image_assets[].id), with exact hashes. shots selects explicit source_in/source_out seconds, role, reason, contain/cover and source_audio_gain. Contain keeps the whole image; cover explicitly permits centre cropping. For still images use source_in=0 and source_out as hold duration.
3. Audio beds have independent source_in, output start, duration, role, gain and fades. Source clip audio defaults to gain 1 when present; silent clips receive explicit silence for composition. Decide when original sound should remain and whether music masks it. For a J/L sound bridge, place a bed from its original source independently of visual clips. The service mixes with limiting; this is not intelligent source separation, auto-ducking, TTS or music generation.
4. For music use music_onsets on an actual registered audio interval ≤300s. It returns source-time spectral onset candidates, not certified beats/BPM. Inspect/listen and select meaningful edges. music_grid uses one increasing source time per shot edge, including opening and ending, and one matching music bed. It records method/reason. Do not treat every transient as a cut.
5. If needed, submit a draft to align_timeline. It rounds music targets to output frames and shortens shots within their approved windows; it cannot extend beyond selected source_out. It returns a draft and explicit changes, not a saved plan. Reinspect shortened actions/holds. Music/voice tracks must fit the new duration; nothing is silently truncated to hide an incompatible plan.
6. Save via save_timeline using fresh project_revision. First plan has supersedes=null; every revision names context.project.timeline_head and gives revision_reason. Request IDs replay only identical content. The immutable record freezes originals, declarations, optional recipe, map and font. Export through export_timeline with plan_id, poll jobs to terminal done, then inspect actual timeline.mp4, map and provenance. Frames are the output clock; quantization and measured grid error are explicit. Native titles use output seconds and are not ASR subtitles.
7. Revisions create new plans/exports. To restore, load the prior plan's request, update request_id/project_revision/supersedes/revision_reason after semantic review, save as a new revision and export. Old files remain. ASR changes do not invalidate a frozen native source-time plan; its source snapshots do not silently follow newer media. A corrupted frozen source/map/font rejects export.
8. Check decoded frame count/duration, contain/crop, real shot identity at edges, original/bed audio timing, meaningful visual holds and actual music transitions. Compare hashes of originals/earlier exports after cut/restore. Preserve first failures and revisions; rendering, schema and waveform checks are not artistic/listening or publication acceptance.

The calling Agent handles commands/services/coordinates and resumes context.timeline_plans. Do not send the user to forms or require remembered commands. Do not publish externally.

## Transitions and real alpha masks

Optional `transitions` selects a non-final `after_shot_id`, kind (`fade`, `wipeleft`, `wiperight`, `slideleft`, `slideright`, `circleopen`), duration and reason. Select a transition because space/time/meaning changes warrant it; ordinary hard cuts remain appropriate. Durations round to output frames and overlap both shots: total frames subtract each overlap. A transition must be shorter than both neighbours, and adjacent transitions must leave an unoverlapped middle interval. At most 32 shots may use this path. Source audio crossfades over the same overlap; independent beds do not restart. Reviewed music edges refer to transition midpoints, or hard-cut edges when no transition applies. Alignment still cannot extend an approved source window.

Optional `masks` composites a registered video/image source with its independent `source_in`, output `start/end`, normalized source `crop` and output `slot`, rectangle/ellipse `shape`, inward `feather` in output pixels (0–64), `opacity` and reason. Alpha is multiplied with existing PNG alpha, preserving transparent holes. Feather cannot exceed half the slot's smaller dimension. Layers apply in list order before titles; this is static source-region composition, without automatic tracking, moving masks, chroma key or AI matting. Inspect both the rendered layer and an unmasked control. Exact source provenance, hashes, immutable revisions and restore apply as before. Old plans without these fields continue to export.
